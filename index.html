How Pangea Venture Executes Your Trade

A transparent, four-stage operational framework designed to eliminate demurrage delays, compliance risks, and supply chain blind spots.

01

Requirement & Feasibility Analysis

We evaluate product specs, target landed cost, Incoterms (FOB/CIF/DDP), HS-code duty structures, and regulatory import permits.

02

Supplier Vetting & Contracting

Shortlisting verified manufacturers or buyers, sample validation, price benchmarking, and secure LC/escrow contract execution.

03

Quality Control & Customs Docs

On-site pre-shipment inspection, container stuffing supervision, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin, and export customs filing.

04

Global Transit & Doorstep Delivery

Live vessel/airway tracking, destination port customs clearance, duty settlement, and final-mile delivery to your warehouse.
